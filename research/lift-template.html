Reconstruct → segment

An image is the intermediate representation.

Image reconstruction

Receives the measurement sequence and measurement model. Passes an estimated two-dimensional image to the task network.

Image-to-task inference

The segmentation head reads the reconstructed image. In this study, it is adapted to the images produced by the reconstruction method.

Image-free segmentation

The task head reads features formed from measurements.

Content-adaptive 1D-to-2D lift

Cross-attention maps the encoded measurement sequence into two-dimensional task features. Their spatial organization is adapted to the measurement content.

Task representation

The segmentation head receives spatial task features. Auxiliary reconstruction supplies training supervision; its reconstructed image is not the segmentation input.

Task-prioritized loss scheduling

Auxiliary reconstruction helps establish structure early in training. The schedule then increases the priority of the segmentation task.
